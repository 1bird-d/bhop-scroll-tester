How it works

  • CS2 has a 15.625 ms jump cooldown. If there's 15.625 ms or less since your last press, the press is ignored, and it still resets the timer. From about 15.87 ms up, every press counts. In between it's a lottery.
  • The bhop window is 7.8125 ms wide, so with a steady gap your perfect-bhop chance is about 7.8125 ÷ gap.
  • Aim for about 16 ms per notch (50–60 notches a second). Scrolling slightly too slow is better than slightly too fast.
  • The game timestamps each scroll by the frame it's processed in, so with a frame cap the gap between presses is always a whole number of frames. Pick your fps_max above and the page snaps your scrolls to that frame grid the same way.

Other tips from the README

  • Set fps_max to a multiple of 62.5 (125, 250, 500…) or slightly below it. Avoid the range just above it, up to a little past the next multiple of 64 (for example 253–256 or 505–512).
  • Don't press or release movement keys right at landing. Finish strafes before you touch down, or after the jump.

Each wheel event counts as one notch. Touchpads, free-spinning wheels and "smooth" or hi-res scrolling send many events per notch, so results with those won't mean much.

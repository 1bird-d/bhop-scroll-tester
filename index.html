How it works

  • CS2 has a 15.625 ms jump cooldown. If there's 15.625 ms or less since your last press, the press is ignored, and it still resets the timer. Above about 15.9 ms, every press counts. In between it's a lottery.
  • The bhop window is 7.8125 ms wide, so with a steady gap your perfect-bhop chance is about 7.8125 ÷ gap.
  • Aim for about 16 ms per notch (50–60 notches a second). Scrolling slightly too slow is better than slightly too fast.

Other tips from the README

  • Set fps_max to a multiple of 62.5 (125, 250, 500…) or slightly below it.
  • Don't press or release movement keys right at landing. Finish strafes before you touch down, or after the jump.

Each wheel event counts as one notch. Touchpads, free-spinning wheels and "smooth" or hi-res scrolling send many events per notch, so results with those won't mean much.
